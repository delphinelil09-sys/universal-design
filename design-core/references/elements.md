# The Eight Elements

Canonical vocabulary. Ordered as formulated, not as a route. For movement order, see `layers.md`.

## 1. Point / Точка — the basis of everything

The smallest unit of attention. Where the eye lands first within a field. Lives in icons, bullets, dividers, focus dots, the period at the end of a statement. Manifests as the smallest anchor the eye can grab.

On a site: favicon, bullet lists, notification dots, the cursor, the dot in a logo, the pulse of an active state.

## 2. Line / Линия — direction and contour

Direction made visible. The trace of a point moving. Lives in rules, underlines, borders, edges, the axis of a grid. Manifests as structure and movement — it tells the eye where to go next.

On a site: horizontal rules between sections, underlines on links, card borders, the invisible grid lines that align everything, the direction of a gaze path.

## 3. Form / Форма — silhouette and geometry

The shape something takes. Where lines close and become an object. Lives in cards, buttons, image crops, containers, avatars. Manifests as identity and hierarchy — a circle and a square say different things before any content arrives.

On a site: button shapes, card corners, image aspect ratios, the silhouette of the hero section, icon geometry.

## 4. Color / Цвет — emotion and tone

The emotional layer. Not decoration — the fastest carrier of mood before any word is read. Lives in background, surface, text, accent. Manifests as tone: warm or cold, calm or urgent, trustworthy or playful.

On a site: background base, surface elevation, text hierarchy, accent for action, state colors (success, error, warning).

## 5. Texture / Текстура — tactility and surface

How a surface would feel if touched. The difference between matte and gloss, smooth and grain, flat and embossed. Lives in backgrounds, image grain, material references, shadow depth. Manifests as warmth and depth — it keeps a page from feeling printed on plastic.

On a site: subtle grain overlays, photographic texture, embossed buttons, paper-like backgrounds, the quality of a shadow (soft vs hard vs none).

## 6. Space / Пространство — air and volume

The gap between things. Not emptiness — a working element. Lives in margins, padding, gaps, line height, the negative area around a focal point. Manifests as rhythm and focus — tight space clusters, wide space separates, and the eye reads the difference immediately.

On a site: section padding, card padding, margin between blocks, line height, the whitespace around a CTA.

## 7. Typography / Типографика — fonts and text

Every word on screen. The voice of the page made visible. Lives in headings, body, labels, buttons, captions. Manifests as voice and readability at once — a typeface carries personality before a single word is understood.

On a site: the type scale, weights, letter spacing, line length, hierarchy between heading levels, the difference between display and body.

## 8. Composition / Композиция — order and balance

The relationship between all of the above. Not an element itself — the meta-element. Where do things sit, what dominates, what recedes, what holds together. Manifests as the page's overall gesture: calm, energetic, editorial, dense, sparse.

On a site: the grid, the alignment system, the reading order, the balance of weight across the page, the relationship between hero and the rest.

---

## How to use this vocabulary

When diagnosing or proposing, name the element you're speaking about. "The color of your CTA fails contrast" is better than "the button looks off" — it locates the problem in a shared language.

When two elements conflict, the one higher on the layers stack wins. See `layers.md`.
